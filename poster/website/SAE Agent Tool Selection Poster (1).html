01

The Tool-Selection Black Box

Modern AI agents autonomously pick among databases, code, web search, and sub-agents. When the agent chooses is observable — why it chose remains opaque.

  • Prompt engineering — correlational, not causal.
  • Behavioural testing — input/output only.
  • Chain-of-thought — plausible, often confabulated.

We open the box at the token where the model commits to a tool, decomposing its hidden state into human-readable features.

03

Pick the Right Layer — Not the MoE Ones

Nemotron-3-Nano-30B is a 52-layer model. A single SAE cannot decompose its MoE layers — reconstruction MSE explodes by >500× at the sparse-routed expert layers.

Fig 2. Bars = alive features; line = reconstruction MSE (log). At the MoE layers MSE jumps to 1,524 (L32) and 508 (L44) — vs. 0.574 at layer 20. Layer 20 sits just before the MoE stack with 81.2% alive features.

JumpReLU Sparse Autoencoder

An overcomplete dictionary that decomposes hidden states into monosemantic features. JumpReLU thresholds θi produce exact zeros — true sparsity — trained with a smooth tanh surrogate.

05

Inside a Feature — Emergent, Unsupervised Concepts

The SAE decomposes Layer-20 activations into 10,752 features. Without contrastive supervision, named concepts emerge from raw activations alone:

“internal knowledge retrieval” “data modification intent” “query complexity”

Each feature carries an LLM-generated label, a token-level fuzzing score (aggregate 0.912 ± 0.008, p < 10⁻⁴), and its row in the contrastive-ablation table.

!

Scope & Limitations

  • One model, one layer, one decision-token convention.
  • Top-10 ablation is conservative; distributed decisions need larger sets.
  • Labels rely on Qwen3-VL-235B — its errors propagate to rationales.

08

Causal Ablation

Prediction-flip rate by contrast type — significance shown per group.

  • Works: fundamental vs. technical — 10.1% flip, controls 0% (p = 0.002).
  • Works: single vs. multi-tool — 17.1% vs. 2.4% baseline (n = 41, p = 0.029).
  • Null: 9 of 23 contrast types show zero flip — encoded distributively, not in a sparse circuit.
Why it's real: random-ablation flip equals SAE round-trip distortion across all 23 types. The contrastive flip sits above this noise floor — from the specific features removed, not encode/decode artefacts.
09

CATE Scaling — Distributed Causal Encoding

Targeted activation patching across expanding feature sets (Top 10/20/30) measures the Conditional Average Treatment Effect. The staircase rise signals abstract tool parameters governed by distributed causal circuits, not localized bottlenecks.

Fig 6. CATE (Δ Prob %) by contrast type across Top-10 / 20 / 30 feature interventions; 500 prompts. The staircase rise with intervention size signals distributed causal encoding. All trajectories clear the Wilcoxon signed-rank test at p < 0.001.
